# Knowledge base and project hub

This repo is Sergio's reference library and the coordination point for his projects. Start every cross-project session here.

## Priorities
1. **Getty Images sales** come first. Judge any change by whether it leads to more accepted, better-captioned, better-found or better-chosen pictures.
2. Less manual work and a bigger audience (blog, London List, Street Name Plates) matter because they feed 1.
3. The archive and knowledge are worth building when they serve 1.

## At the start of a session
- Read `hub/backlog.md` (open decisions and ranked work) and `hub/projects.md` (how the repos connect).
- Repos: `on-foot-blog`, `london-list`, `street-name-plates`, `photo-metadata-tool`, `knowledge-base`. The Getty pipeline itself runs in the claude.ai **Getty Images Portfolio** project. Its instructions win over the Getty files here until the rulebook is settled (review §1).

## At the end of a session
- Update `hub/backlog.md`: change statuses, add new items with an owner, move finished items to Done with the date.
- Larger findings go in a dated note under `hub/` (e.g. `review-2027-02.md`), not in chat only.

## Conventions
- British English, direct tone, no marketing language.
- Work on branches and open a PR for Sergio to approve from his phone. Never merge.
- **This repo is public.** Don't add sales amounts, customer names, financial files, guides' or members of the public's names, or anything from the Mac's private planner data.
- Don't move or rename existing files without asking: their raw URLs may be pasted into claude.ai projects.
